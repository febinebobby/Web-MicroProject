Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding Choice Based Credit System (CBCS), grade calculation rules, and continuous evaluation policies.

SGPA is the weighted average of grade points obtained across all registered courses in a semester. It is computed as:

SGPA = Σ(Credit Hours × Grade Points) / Σ(Total Credits)

Each course is assigned credits (usually 1 to 5), and the grade point corresponding to your letter grade (e.g., O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8) is multiplied by the course credits.

To earn credits for a registered course, a student must secure at least 50% marks in aggregate (B Grade, 6.0 Grade Points) combining continuous assessments and end-semester evaluations. Securing below 50% results in a RA (Re-Appear) standing requiring re-examination.

The standard CIA weightage model comprises:

  • CIA Test 1: 25 Marks
  • CIA Test 2: 25 Marks
  • Continuous Quizzes & Assignments: 20 Marks
  • Model Exam / Practical: 30 Marks

This ensures consistent academic engagement throughout the semester rather than reliance solely on final examinations.

In accordance with AICTE and Karunya university standards, equivalent percentage is derived through the standard conversion formula:

Percentage (%) = (CGPA − 0.5) × 10

For example, an SGPA/CGPA of 9.24 yields: (9.24 − 0.5) × 10 = 87.4%.

Karunya regulations require a minimum of 75% overall attendance in each course. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% are eligible only under sanctioned medical condonation. The live dashboard actively flags attendance below threshold.
