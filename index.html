Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to grading guidelines, credit distributions, and performance calculations at Karunya Institute.

SGPA is computed using the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) weighted average formula: SGPA = Σ(Course Credits × Grade Points Earned) / Σ(Total Registered Credits). Each course letter grade corresponds to a university-defined grade point (e.g. O = 10.0, A+ = 9.0, A = 8.0, B+ = 7.0, B = 6.0, RA = 0.0).

CIA is a comprehensive evaluation structure comprising internal assessments: CIA-1 (25%), CIA-2 (25%), Quizzes / Assignments / Lab Assessments (20%), and the End-Semester Examination (30%). This ensures students are evaluated consistently across the semester.

To graduate with First Class with Distinction, a student must secure a cumulative CGPA of 8.50 or higher, clear all courses in the first attempt without any backlogs, and complete the degree program within the standard stipulated duration.

A total score below 50 marks results in an RA (Re-Appear / Arrear) grade with 0.0 grade points. The student is flagged for remedial mentorship sessions and must re-appear during supplementary or subsequent semester examinations.

Yes. In compliance with university and UGC standards, the equivalent percentage is computed as Percentage (%) = (SGPA - 0.5) × 10. Our interactive calculator displays this instantly for placement and higher education readiness.
