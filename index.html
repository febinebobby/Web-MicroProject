Core Capabilities

Our Key Features

Engineered to eliminate manual grading friction and provide students and faculty with crystal-clear academic clarity.

✏️

Modular Marks Entry

Faculty can seamlessly record student marks across all assessment formats. Built specifically around Karunya's continuous assessment structure.

  • Enter marks by course code & subject
  • Continuous Internal Assessment (CIA-1 & CIA-2)
  • Assignment, lab practical & end-sem inputs
  • Instant edit and quick draft saving
🧮

Automated Grade Calculation

No more manual calculation spreadsheets. The system computes SGPA, CGPA, and letter grades based on credit weightage and standard university thresholds.

  • Instant 10-point scale conversion
  • Automated SGPA & cumulative CGPA
  • Official letter grades (O, A+, A, B+, B, C, F)
  • Arrear detection & credit validation
📊

Performance Analytics

Holistic visual analytics for mentors and students. Identify strengths, track historical progression, and flag areas requiring remedial coaching.

  • Subject-wise distribution breakdown
  • Early alert for at-risk performance
  • Class average & cohort benchmark
  • One-click printable academic summaries

System Workflow

How The Platform Works

From internal continuous evaluation to semester transcripts in four simple steps.

1

Select Subject & Class

Faculty picks the department, degree program, semester, and course catalog from the central directory.

2

Upload or Key-In Marks

Input CIA-1, CIA-2, lab assignments, or upload batch marks directly through Excel spreadsheets.

3

Automated Audit & Calculation

Algorithms verify credit weights, calculate SGPA/CGPA, and assign letter grades in milliseconds.

4

Publish & Review Insights

Students access their verified grades while mentors inspect class trends and remedial recommendations.
