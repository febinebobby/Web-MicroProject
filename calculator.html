CBCS Formula Reference & Viva Insights

Official mathematical formulas for Choice Based Credit System (CBCS)
Regulation 2024

1. Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)

The weighted average of grade points earned in all courses registered during the semester:

SGPA = Σ(Ci × GPi) / ΣCi
Where Ci = Course Credits, GPi = Grade Points earned (0.0 to 10.0).

2. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

The cumulative weighted average of all credits and grade points earned across all completed semesters:

CGPA = Σ(Ck × GPk) / ΣCk
Computed at the end of every semester until degree conferral.

3. Percentage Equivalence Formula

AICTE & Karunya standard conversion formula from 10-point scale to equivalent marks percentage:

Percentage (%) = (SGPA − 0.5) × 10
Example: SGPA 9.24 equates to (9.24 - 0.5) × 10 = 87.4%.
